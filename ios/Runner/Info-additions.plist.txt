Add these keys inside the <dict> of ios/Runner/Info.plist (they don't exist by
default in a fresh `flutter create` project — merge them in, don't replace the file):

<key>NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription</key>
<string>We use your location once to save your office as a geofence.</string>

<key>NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription</key>
<string>Background location lets us detect when you arrive at or leave the office, even when the app is closed.</string>

<key>UIBackgroundModes</key>
<array>
  <string>location</string>
</array>

Also in ios/Podfile, make sure the platform line is:
platform :ios, '14.0'
